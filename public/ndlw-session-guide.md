## 🧠 Core Concepts (Preview)

### 1. Cognitive Diversity as Educational Advantage
Different brains process information differently:
- **Detail-focused processing** (often autistic learners): Notice small patterns first, build to big picture
- **Gestalt processing** (often neurotypical learners): See overall pattern first, may miss subtle details
- **Neither is "better"**—they're complementary and strengthen learning communities

### 2. Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
The CAST framework provides three principles:
- **Multiple Means of Representation**: Provide information in visual, text, audio formats
- **Multiple Means of Action/Expression**: Allow students to demonstrate learning through various modalities
- **Multiple Means of Engagement**: Offer choice, relevance, collaboration options

**Key Insight:** Design for the margins benefits everyone (curb-cut effect)

### 3. AI as Cognitive Prosthetic
Just as eyeglasses extend vision, AI can:
- **Reduce cognitive load**: Summarize readings, break down complex tasks
- **Support executive function**: Task planning, time management, organization
- **Provide scaffolding**: Step-by-step guidance, just-in-time support
- **Generate multiple formats**: Same content as text, diagram, video, audio

### 4. Structured Freedom Framework
Clear constraints enable creativity:
- **Clear learning objectives** (what students need to know)
- **Defined success criteria** (how they demonstrate mastery)
- **Multiple pathways** (choice in how to learn and express learning)
- **Safe-to-fail environment** (low-stakes practice)

## ❓ Common Questions (Addressed in Session)

**"Won't this lower academic standards?"**  
→ No—UDL provides access to learning, not reduced rigor. Students still meet the same learning objectives.

**"This sounds like a lot of extra work."**  
→ Yes, initially. But once designed, it scales. Start with one module.

**"What about students who might game the system?"**  
→ Trust is a separate issue from accommodation. Most students appreciate flexibility.

**"How do I balance flexibility with structure?"**  
→ Structured freedom framework: Clear objectives + multiple pathways.
